### Key Features

1. **Duplicate Prevention**
   - GradeDataInitializer checks for existing grades before inserting
   - Safe to re-run without data duplication

2. **Auto-Calculated Fields**
   - Percentage: Calculated from marks
   - Letter Grade: A+, A, B+, B, C, D, F based on percentage
   - Grade Point: 10.0, 9.0, 8.0, 7.0, 6.0, 5.0, 0.0 based on grade

3. **Ordered Execution**
   - @Order(3): Subjects load first
   - @Order(4): Timetables load second
   - @Order(5): Grades load last
   - Prevents foreign key violations
